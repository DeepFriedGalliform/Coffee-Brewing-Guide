GRINDER

Recipes are written for the Fellow Ode Gen 2. Other grinders get converted settings, which are approximate starting points: adjust to taste, then use the drawdown advice after each brew.

CUSTOM GRINDER

Enter the finest and coarsest settings on your grinder's dial, and the size of one click or step (e.g. 1, or 0.1). Every recipe is scaled across that range.

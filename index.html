Settings

GRINDER

Recipes are written for the Fellow Ode Gen 2. Other grinders get converted settings, which are approximate starting points: adjust to taste, then use the drawdown advice after each brew.

CUSTOM GRINDER

Enter the finest and coarsest settings on your grinder's dial, and the size of one click or step (e.g. 1, or 0.1). Every recipe is scaled across that range.

BACKUP

Your settings are stored only on this device. Export saves your coffees, recipes, usual settings, method, brew size, grinder, grind settings and display options, so you can restore them on a new phone or after reinstalling. Importing replaces your current settings.

STEPS

Pour to is the scale reading at the end of a pour, as a % of the total water. Times are given for 250 g and 500 g of water, and other brew sizes are scaled between them. Finish with a Drawdown step.
